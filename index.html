Every group, day by day

Live

One column per group, Racers I through Senior — the same order as the old table, but for the actual day. The arrows move day by day, so a Saturday shows only the groups that swim Saturday.

Your swimmer’s week

Live

Pick your swimmer’s group under Teams and the week shows only its practices, Saturdays included. Subscribe puts that group in your phone’s calendar, or emails you when its schedule changes.

What this replaces

  • A table titled “Fall 2024”. The live page still says the schedule begins Tuesday, September 4, 2024. A family can’t tell whether those times still hold. These calendars open on this week and are the schedule the coaches keep now.
  • No dates at all. A weekday table can’t say when the season ends or that a holiday is off. The calendar leaves out the days there is no practice, and the line under the heading says how far the schedule runs.
  • Retyping every season. Each change means editing a table cell by hand. Here a coach changes the practice once, in Pool Relay, and this page, the pool’s schedule and every subscribed phone follow.
  • Changes nobody hears about. When a practice moves, families who subscribed to their group get the change in their calendar and an email, without anyone posting it.
  • Phones. A seven-column table is a sideways scroll on a phone. On a narrow screen the calendar opens as a list.
